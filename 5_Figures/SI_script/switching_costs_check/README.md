# Switching costs: replay of the membership decisions and a geopolitical schedule (8 October 2026)

`replay.py` replays the decisions of a V32 membership process from its result csv (`4_DB\Database_..._agg_forest_<tag>.csv`: EV by region and iteration, membership by iteration) with the experience rule of `CGE_Game_Theory_GTAPAEZ_all_V32.R` (a member leaves when its EV is more than its threshold below what it last had outside, or under the 0.1 %-of-GDP safeguard; an outsider joins when its EV is more than its threshold below what it last had inside, or below the previous round if it was never inside). Run as is, it reproduces the recorded membership path of every run it is given (validation), and it reports, for every decision, the measure the rule compared with the threshold and the margin. Under another threshold schedule it reports the first iteration at which the path would differ. It cannot go further than the first divergence: from there a new run is needed.

`geo_schedule.py` builds a geopolitical schedule from the UN General Assembly ideal points of Bailey, Strezhnev and Voeten (2019–2023 means, from un-project.org/api/countries/<iso3>/ideal-points/, re-centred per year): s_r = max(0, x_EU − x_r), the one-sided distance below the EU on the liberal-order axis; g_r = 0.5 + s_r / (2 s_median) (0.5 for the regions on the Western side of the EU, 1 at the median, 1.55 for Venezuela); thresholds = trade schedule × g_r (symmetric), or join × g_r and leave / g_r (asymmetric). `compare.py` replays the headline runs under both and re-reads the one-deviation tests (`4_DB\Stability_*.txt`) with the new thresholds. Outputs: `geo_schedule_output.txt`, `compare_output.txt`, `knife_edge_output.txt` (the decisions within 10 / 25 / 50 % of their threshold under the paper's schedule).

Needs the model folder (`FOREST_CLUB_REPO` or three levels up), `openpyxl`.
