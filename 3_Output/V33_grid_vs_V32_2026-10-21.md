# V33 grid (phase 1) against V32 — 21 October 2026

Source: `4_DB/V33_runs_2026-10-20.txt` (26 runs, 20–21 Oct, 172 solves in 12 h, faces 7–10, one pass each), the 25 v33 `agg_forest` csv files in `4_DB`, `4_DB/V32_runs_2026-10-07.txt` and `5_Figures/data/all_data_V32.json`. Welfare figures are the batch's net measure (EV net of the wedge term, relative to the EUDR world), M USD per year. "Kept" is the BAU clearing the members hold, tropical, Mha; "leakage" the extra clearing of the outsiders.

## 1. Bottom line

The game is structurally the same. On 23 of the 25 grid points that ran, V33 gives the same outcome as V32 (club forms or not, Brazil inside or outside at convergence), the same membership to within one or two small regions, the same number of rounds (±1), the same joining round for Brazil, China, the US, Japan and India, and the same money within 3–10 %. The thresholds of the penalty (1.25 %), of the EU + China seeds (0.87 % and 1,000 USD/ha) and of the Mercosur seeds (no charge) are unchanged.

Two grid points flipped, both at the floor of the surcharge: EU alone at 2,000 USD/ha (club with Brazil in V32, Brazil outside in V33 — the surcharge floor moves one step up, to 2,500) and the combined 0.62 % + 1,000 USD/ha (worked, now fails — the combined floor at 1,000 USD/ha moves to 0.87 %). Both flips come from a single knife-edge, Colombia's first-round loss against its switching cost of 10.85 M USD: 10.9 in V32 (Colombia joins in round 2), 10.7–10.8 in V33 (stays out). Without Colombia in the round-2 club the next layer does not come: at 2,000 USD/ha MENA's round-2 fall is 50 instead of 99 M USD (threshold 83) and North Africa's 33 instead of 40 (threshold 39); at 0.62 % + 1,000 China's is 129 instead of 220 (threshold 215) and the US's 157 instead of 200 (threshold 196). Without those, Brazil's loss outside never reaches its threshold and the club stays small. These V32 results were themselves chains of margins of 0.5–2 %; wherever Brazil joins on its own first-round loss — the mechanism the paper narrates — V32 and V33 agree (the first-round losses differ by 2–3 %).

Systematic shifts, all in the direction the calibration implies:

- Leakage at the full club: 0.73 Mha instead of 0.54 (+35 %), entirely Angola (0.50 vs 0.31 Mha; Angola AEZ5 now carries −0.77 instead of −0.25, three times the default response). Leakage is 4.9 % of the forest kept instead of 3.6 %. CAFR (0.15), Colombia (0.04) and Venezuela (0.01) are unchanged; the DRC's leakage is nil (−0.002 vs −0.014).
- Brazil's gain as a member: +9 % (1,144 vs 1,048 at 1.5 %; gross 1,372 vs 1,152 — the wedge term doubled with the AEZ6 wedge, 226 → 1,666 %). EU and US costs +3 to +5 % (−1,262 vs −1,220; −859 vs −825), China within 3 %, world net unchanged (−2,729 vs −2,718). Brazil's first-round losses +2–3 % everywhere (105.6 vs 102.6 M USD at 1.5 %).
- Bolivia (1.09 Mha of BAU clearing, the third-largest) no longer stays in any club below 6,000 USD/ha. Its EV as a member is −31 to −35 M USD (V32: −4 to −8) against a switching cost of 2 M USD; it tries in round 3 or 4 and leaves, as Angola, Colombia, CAFR and Venezuela do in both versions. This is why "kept" is 14.94 Mha at every penalty in V33 while V32 had 16.02 at 2.5 and 5 % (Bolivia inside), and why the surcharge coalitions keep about 1 Mha less (11.65 at 2,500 vs 12.65). Bolivia's own cells did not change (−0.25 in both, wedges 0.90–0.95 of V32); the change comes through the general equilibrium — its Stage A welfare moved from −10 to +15 M USD, clearing is worth more to it now. Bolivia was already a marginal member in V32; worth one look at `Welfare_decomp`, not a structural change.
- The DRC no longer tests membership at 1.25–1.5 % (V32: joined in round 4–5 at a cost of −105 and left); at 2,000–3,500 USD/ha it still tries in round 2 and leaves (−56 vs −101). Its loss outside at round 3 is 0.7 M USD below its 2 M USD floor instead of 2.2 above — the floor is the resolution of the game.
- Japan is in at 1.25, 2.5 and 5 % and out at 1.5 % — the same pattern in both versions. Myanmar is now a member at 1.25 % (36 members instead of 35). At 1.5 % the 35 members are identical.

## 5. The headline charges

The paper's 1.5 % and 4,000 USD/ha were the V30 minima of 30 Sept. Three ways to set the V33 headline:

- Rule 1 — the lowest grid charge at which the club forms (Brazil inside at convergence): **1.25 % and 2,500 USD/ha**. At 2,500 Brazil joins in round 4, after MENA, North Africa, South Asia, India, Japan, Korea, Russia and the UK enter in round 3; the result rests on margins of 5 % (South Asia) and 9 % (MENA). The V32 equivalent, 2,000, rested on margins of 0.5 % (Colombia) and 2 % (North Africa) and flipped.
- Rule 2 — the lowest grid charge at which Brazil joins on its own first-round loss: **1.25 %** (20 % above its threshold) and **3,500 USD/ha** (2.4 % above; 0.4 % in V32) — or **4,000** for a margin of 15 %.
- Continuity — keep 1.5 % and 4,000 (margins 44 % and 15 %).

Recommendation: 1.25 % and 4,000 USD/ha — Rule 2 with a margin of at least 10 %. It is robust to the baseline change (V32 and V33 give the same answer at every grid point where Brazil joins on its own), it is the mechanism the text already narrates (Brazil joins in the second round because its first-round loss exceeds its switching cost, then the cascade), it keeps the surcharge chapter (lock-outs, Table S9, the plane) at 4,000, and the SI can state the rule and the margins in one sentence. 1.25 % is the minimum under either rule; the 1.25 % club has 36 members (Japan and Myanmar in; outsiders Angola, Bolivia, CAFR, Colombia, DRC, Venezuela), keeps 14.94 Mha with 0.73 Mha of leakage, world −2.7 bn, Brazil +1.14 bn, EU −1.27 bn, US −0.87 bn, China −0.80 bn — the same money as the 1.5 % club. If the strict minimum is preferred, 3,500 works in both versions but a 2.4 % margin is within what a calibration choice moves (the first-round losses moved 2–3 % between V32 and V33).

## 7. What this means for the text

Nothing in the paper's storyline changes: the club forms from 1.25 % and cascades to 35–36 members; the surcharge needs 2,500–4,000 USD/ha depending on the rule and brings in 18–24 members; the EU + China seed lowers both thresholds; the Mercosur seed needs no charge; Brazil gains, the EU, the US and China pay; the one-step-from-the-cap leakage stays a few per cent of the forest kept. Numbers to redo once phase 2 is in: every figure in the Results and SI (headline 1.25 % instead of 1.5 %; surcharge chapter at 4,000 or 2,500), the leakage sentences (0.7 Mha, 5 %, Angola two-thirds of it), the Bolivia mentions (no longer a member of the 2.5–5 % clubs or of the surcharge coalitions below 6,000), the DRC and Japan round-by-round sentences, Table S5b (the calibration), and the thresholds table (surcharge floor 2,500, combined floor 0.87 % at 1,000 USD/ha, Brazil's own-loss crossings).
